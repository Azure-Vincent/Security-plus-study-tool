# SY0-801 compared with SY0-701

This app teaches the **SY0-701** objectives. CompTIA is replacing that exam with **SY0-801** (Security+ V8). This page records what is known about the change and how it affects the app.

> **Status: reported, not yet confirmed against CompTIA’s own documents.** comptia.org could not be reached when this page was written (October 2026), so the details below come from third-party summaries. Those sources agree on the domain weights and the AI content, but disagree on whether the objectives document is final or still marked “under development”. Check the official SY0-801 objectives before relying on any detail here.

## Dates

| | Reported date |
|---|---|
| SY0-801 English launch | About November 17, 2026 |
| SY0-701 English retirement | About June 11, 2027 (other languages later) |
| Overlap when either exam can be booked | About November 2026 to June 2027 |

## Domains

| Domain | SY0-701 | SY0-801 (reported) | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1.0 General Security Concepts | 12% | 16% | +4 points |
| 2.0 Threats, Vulnerabilities, and Mitigations → Threats, Vulnerabilities, and Attacks (reported name) | 22% | 24% | +2 points, renamed |
| 3.0 Security Architecture | 18% | 19% | +1 point |
| 4.0 Security Operations | 28% | 27% | −1 point |
| 5.0 Security Program Management and Oversight | 20% | 14% | −6 points |

The five-domain structure stays the same. Weight moves from governance and program management (domain 5) toward core concepts (domain 1) and threats (domain 2).

## Content changes reported so far

- **New: AI security risks.** A new objective on the security risks of artificial intelligence is reported under the threats domain. SY0-701 has no AI objective, and this app does not yet cover it.
- **Shift away from governance.** Domain 5 drops from 20% to 14%, so expect fewer questions on policies, third-party risk, compliance and audits.
- **More emphasis on fundamentals and threats.** Domains 1 and 2 grow together by six points.

## What this means for this app

- **Taking SY0-701 before it retires:** the app covers every SY0-701 objective, and nothing here changes that.
- **Taking SY0-801:** almost all of the material still applies, because most objectives carry over. Two things to adjust:
  - Treat the mock exams’ domain weighting as approximate, since they use SY0-701 weights.
  - Study AI security risks separately until the app covers them.
